What RotaWake does

Six things, done properly, with nothing you have to maintain by hand.

Any rotation, any cycle length

4 on 4 off, Panama, continental, days and nights, weekdays — or build your own cycle in under a minute. RotaWake stores the rule, not a list of dates, so it never runs out.

Your whole month at a glance

Colour-coded shifts with today outlined and your next shift pinned above the grid. Scroll forwards or backwards as far as you like.

A reminder before every shift

Set how far ahead you want the warning and it arrives on time, with no connection and no account. Android schedules it in advance.

Change one day without breaking the pattern

Cover a shift, book leave or mark a training day. The rotation carries on underneath, so the days after it are untouched.

Hours, overtime and earnings

Monthly totals by shift type, a breakdown per shift, and export to a spreadsheet or your calendar. Part of RotaWake Pro.

No account, no cloud, no tracking

Everything is worked out on your phone. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak.

Common questions

How do I set up my rotation?

Pick the pattern that matches your rota, then tap which day of the cycle you are on today. RotaWake works out every other day from there, forwards and backwards, for as far as you scroll.

Does it work offline?

Yes. RotaWake has no account and no server. Everything is worked out on your phone, so it behaves exactly the same with no signal at all.

My reminders arrive late. What do I change?

Android battery optimisation is almost always the cause. Open your phone’s Settings, find RotaWake under Apps, and set its battery usage to Unrestricted.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a single payment and it is yours permanently. There are no renewals and no trial that turns into a bill.
