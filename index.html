What Rotaday does

Calendar

The month at a glance, with your next shift on top.

  • Every future date filled in from your pattern
  • Day, night, evening and leave, each with its own colour
  • The next shift with a countdown
  • Hours and shift count for the month
Patterns and rosters

Whatever rotation you actually work.

  • Built in: 4 on 4 off, Panama (2-2-3), 4 days 4 nights, Monday to Friday
  • Build a custom cycle of any length
  • Pin a pattern to the week so it never drifts
  • Add a new roster when you change job, keeping old months intact
Changes and notes

Real weeks never match the rota exactly.

  • Swap a single day to another shift type
  • Mark a day off without touching the pattern
  • Add a note to any date
  • Changes are counted separately in reports
Shift types

Your hours, your rates.

  • Start, end and break for each type
  • Overnight shifts handled correctly
  • An hourly rate per shift type
  • Eight colours, each with readable label text
Reports (Pro)

What the month actually adds up to.

  • Total hours and shifts
  • Overtime against your contracted week
  • Earnings by shift type
  • Export a month as CSV, or add it to your phone calendar
Reminders and privacy

Quiet, private and yours.

  • A reminder 30 minutes to 4 hours before a shift
  • A 4-digit PIN lock
  • Back up and restore from a file
  • No account and no cloud

Common questions

Which patterns are built in?

4 on 4 off, Panama (2-2-3), four days then four nights, and Monday to Friday. You can also build a cycle of any length.

What if my rotation changes?

Add a new roster from the date it starts. Earlier months keep the old pattern, so your history stays accurate.

Can I swap one day?

Yes. Tap the date, pick another shift type or mark it off. The rest of the pattern is untouched.

How far ahead can I look?

As far as you like. The calendar is calculated from your pattern, so any future month is available.
