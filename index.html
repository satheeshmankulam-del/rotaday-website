What RotaWake does

Four things, done properly, with nothing you have to maintain by hand.

Any rotation

4 on 4 off, Panama, continental, days and nights, weekdays — or build your own cycle in under a minute.

Months ahead

Scroll forwards or backwards as far as you like. Every shift is worked out from the pattern, not stored one by one.

Shift reminders

A notification before every shift starts, and you choose how far ahead the warning arrives.

Change one day

Cover a shift, book leave or mark a training day. The rotation carries on underneath, untouched.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account. No sign-up. No cloud, no analytics, no advertising. Your roster is stored on your device and nowhere else.
