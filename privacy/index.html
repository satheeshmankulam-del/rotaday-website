Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026. Applies to the Rotaday Android app (com.satheesh.roster) and this website.

In short: Rotaday does not collect, upload or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and only you can see it.

1. Information you enter

To work, the app stores what you set up:

  • Your patterns, rosters and the date each one starts
  • Shift types, with their times, breaks and hourly rates
  • Single-day changes and notes you add to dates
  • Settings, including reminders, contracted hours, currency and your PIN

All of it is saved on your device. It is never sent to the developer, a server, a cloud service or any third party.

2. Pay information

Hourly rates and the earnings estimates built from them stay on your device only. The developer cannot access, view or recover them, and Rotaday never connects to an employer, a payroll system or a bank.

3. Permissions

Rotaday asks only for what its features need. No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

  • Notifications: to remind you before a shift starts, if you turn reminders on.
  • Alarms and reminders: so a reminder arrives at the time you chose.
  • Files and sharing: only when you back up, restore or export a CSV.
  • Calendar: only if you choose to add a month to your phone's calendar.

4. Internet, analytics and third parties

The app works fully offline and sends none of your data anywhere. It contains no advertising or analytics tools.

The one-time Pro unlock is handled by Google Play billing, which processes the payment. Rotaday never sees your payment details, and Google's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

5. Backups

Back up to a file creates a JSON file, and Export as CSV creates a spreadsheet file. You choose where each one goes through Android's share sheet. They contain your rota and pay rates, so share them only with people you trust.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. Reset everything in Settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently erases it. Because there is no account or server, no copy is held anywhere else.

7. Security

Your information is protected by your phone's own security. You can also turn on a 4-digit PIN lock in Settings. There is no way to recover a forgotten PIN, so pick one you will remember.

8. Children's privacy

Rotaday is a tool for working adults and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. No data is collected from any user.

9. This website

This website doesn't use forms or accounts. It may use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and clicks. That information is not linked to the app or to anything you enter in it. See the Microsoft privacy statement.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
