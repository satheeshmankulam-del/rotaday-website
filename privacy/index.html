Privacy Policy

RotaWake (formerly Rotaday) · package com.satheesh.roster · last updated 18 October 2026

RotaWake does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

This policy covers the Android app RotaWake, published by Satheesh Mankulam under the package name com.satheesh.roster. The app was previously called Rotaday; the name changed but the app, its package and this policy did not.

What RotaWake stores

Your shift patterns, rosters, shift types, day changes, notes and settings are saved in the app’s own storage on your device. They are not uploaded, backed up to any server, or shared with anyone.

No account

There is no sign-up and no login. RotaWake never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal detail.

No tracking or advertising

RotaWake contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your use of the app is not measured or reported.

No internet connection

RotaWake works entirely offline. It does not send or receive data over the internet.

Notifications

Shift reminders are scheduled by your phone’s own Android system and shown on the device. No reminder information leaves your phone.

Purchases

If RotaWake offers a paid upgrade, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. RotaWake receives only whether a purchase exists. Your payment details are never seen by the app or its developer.

Files you create

Backup and export files are created only when you ask for one, and go wherever you choose to send them. Once a file leaves the app, it is governed by whichever service you shared it with.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from Settings, then Reset everything. Uninstalling RotaWake also removes all of its data from your phone. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, there is nothing left to delete.

Children

RotaWake is a work scheduling tool intended for adults. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to satheeshmankulam@gmail.com.
