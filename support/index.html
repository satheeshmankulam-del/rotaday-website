Setting up your rota

Open Settings, then Patterns, and pick the rotation you work. Then open Rosters and set the date you're currently on in the cycle. The calendar fills in from there.

If the shifts look shifted by a day or two, adjust which day of the cycle today is, and the whole calendar moves with it.

Reminders aren't arriving

Turn reminders on in Settings and choose how far ahead you want them, from 30 minutes to 4 hours.

If they still don't arrive, Android may be limiting the app in the background. Allow notifications for Rotaday, set its battery use to unrestricted, and on phones from brands such as Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme or OnePlus, turn on Auto-start.

Back up and move to a new phone

  1. Open Settings and tap Back up to a file. Android's share sheet lets you save it to Drive, email it to yourself or keep it on the phone.
  2. On the new phone, install Rotaday and tap Restore from a file.

Before you uninstall: uninstalling erases your rosters, changes and notes permanently. Back up first.

Delete your data

Rotaday has no account and no servers, so everything is on your phone.

Open Settings and tap Reset everything in the Danger zone, or uninstall the app. Both permanently erase your rosters, changes, notes and settings.
