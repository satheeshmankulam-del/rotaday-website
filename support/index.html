Before you write

These three cover most of what comes in:

Reminders arrive late or not at all

Android battery optimisation. Open Settings on your phone, find RotaWake under Apps, set battery usage to Unrestricted, and allow exact alarms if your phone asks. Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and OnePlus are the strictest.

Pro is locked after reinstalling or changing phone

Settings, then Restore purchase, signed in to the Google account you bought it with. If it still does not unlock, send me the order number from your Google Play receipt.

My pattern starts on the wrong day

Open the pattern and tap the day of the cycle you are on today. That one tap re-anchors the whole rotation.

Useful when reporting a problem

  • Your phone make and model
  • Your Android version
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of the About screen
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
