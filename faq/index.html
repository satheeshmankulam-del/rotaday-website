Patterns and rosters

Which patterns are built in?

4 on 4 off, Panama (2-2-3), four days then four nights, and Monday to Friday. You can also build a cycle of any length.

What if my rotation changes?

Add a new roster from the date it starts. Earlier months keep the old pattern, so your history stays accurate.

Can I swap one day?

Yes. Tap the date, pick another shift type or mark it off. The rest of the pattern is untouched.

How far ahead can I look?

As far as you like. The calendar is calculated from your pattern, so any future month is available.

Hours, pay and reports

Does Rotaday work out my hours?

Yes. Each shift type has a start, end and break, so the app totals hours per month, including overnight shifts.

How is overtime calculated?

Set your contracted hours per week, and anything above that is counted as overtime. Left at zero, the app shows no overtime at all.

Can it estimate my pay?

Yes, if you set an hourly rate per shift type. It's an estimate for your own planning, not a payslip.

Can I export a month?

Yes. Export a month as CSV, or add it to your phone's calendar.

General

Is Rotaday free?

The app is free to use. Reports, covering hours, overtime and earnings, come with a one-time Pro unlock. There is no subscription.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything is calculated and stored on your phone.

Does it show ads?

No. There are no ads and no tracking.
