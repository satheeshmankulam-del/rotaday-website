Questions

If none of these cover it, get in touch — a real person reads every message.

How do I set up my rotation?

Pick the pattern that matches your rota, then tap which day of the cycle you are on today. RotaWake works out every other day from there, forwards and backwards, for as far as you scroll.

What if my pattern isn’t in the list?

Build your own. Choose a cycle length, then tap each day in the cycle to set it to a day, evening, night or rest shift. Any cycle length works.

Does it work offline?

Yes. RotaWake has no account and no server. Everything is worked out on your phone, so it behaves exactly the same with no signal at all.

Will reminders still arrive without a connection?

Yes. RotaWake hands your next few weeks of shifts to Android in advance, so reminders arrive with no connection and no account.

My reminders arrive late. What do I change?

Android battery optimisation is almost always the cause. Open your phone’s Settings, find RotaWake under Apps, and set its battery usage to Unrestricted. On some phones you also need to allow exact alarms.

Can I change a single day without breaking the pattern?

Yes. Tap the day and pick a different shift, mark leave, or add a note. The rotation carries on underneath, so the following days are unaffected.

What does Pro include?

Hours, overtime and earnings totals by month, a breakdown by shift type, backup and restore, and spreadsheet export.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a single payment and it is yours permanently. There are no renewals and no trial that turns into a bill.

I bought Pro but it is locked on my new phone.

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Google account you bought it with. Your purchase is tied to that account, not to the device.

How do I back up my roster?

Settings, then Back up my roster. That writes a file you can send wherever you like. Restoring reads the same file back.

Does RotaWake collect any data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you enter leaves your phone. The privacy policy says so in full.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then Reset everything. Uninstalling also removes all of its data. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, there is nothing left behind.
