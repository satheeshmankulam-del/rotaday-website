A shift calendar for 4 on 4 off and other rotas

Rotaday takes your rotation, such as 4 on 4 off, Panama or four days then four nights, and fills in every future date for you. No more counting days on a paper calendar or copying the rota into a phone calendar each month.

Updated September 2026

Why rotating shifts are hard to keep track of

A rota that repeats every eight, twelve or fourteen days never lines up with the week. Which Saturday you're off changes each month, and the printed rota often covers only a few weeks. Planning anything two months out means counting cycles by hand.

How Rotaday works

  • Pick your pattern from the built-in ones, or build your own cycle of any length.
  • Set day one by telling the app which date you're on in the cycle.
  • Every future date fills in from there, as far ahead as you want to look.
  • Swap a single day when you cover for someone, without breaking the pattern.
  • Add a roster when you change job or rotation, and earlier months keep the old one.

What else it tracks

Each shift type carries its own start, end, break and hourly rate, so Rotaday can total your hours, flag anything over your contracted week as overtime, and estimate earnings by shift type. A reminder can arrive 30 minutes to 4 hours before a shift starts, and a month can be exported as CSV or pushed to your phone's calendar.

Questions

Which shift patterns are built in?

4 on 4 off, Panama (2-2-3), four days then four nights, and Monday to Friday. You can also build a cycle of any length yourself.

What if I swap a shift with a colleague?

Tap that date and pick a different shift type, or mark it off. Only that day changes; the pattern carries on.

What happens when my rotation changes?

Add a new roster from the date the change starts. Earlier months keep showing the old pattern, so your history stays correct.

Is Rotaday free?

The app is free to use. Reports, which cover hours, overtime and earnings, are part of a one-time Pro unlock, with no subscription.
